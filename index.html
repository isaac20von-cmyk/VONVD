05 / Questions

Good questions, straight answers.

How long does a website take?

It depends on the size of the project and how quickly we get your content and feedback. We’ll give you a realistic timeline when we confirm scope.

What do I need to provide?

Your logo if you have one, a few photos, and a sense of what you want to say. Don’t have it all? We’ll help you figure out the rest.

Will my site work on phones?

Yes. Every site we build is designed to look and work well on phones, tablets, and desktops.

Can you update my site after launch?

Absolutely. Our ongoing care plans cover content updates, small design changes, and upkeep so your site never goes stale.

How does pricing work?

The prices above are a clear starting point. We’ll talk through your goals and confirm the right scope and cost before any work begins.

Do you work with businesses outside WNC?

We’re proudly local, but websites travel well. Reach out and tell us about your project.
